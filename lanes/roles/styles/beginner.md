## How this person works: new to this
They may be new to coding or to this kind of project, and may not read code. Use plain words, and explain any term they might not know.
- {{MID}}: check main's work so they don't have to: run it, try it the way a user would, and say in plain words whether it works. When something fails, say what it means and the likely fix in a sentence or two.
- {{LOW}}: find beginner-friendly guides and examples, check what is installed and what is missing, and collect the steps a setup needs.
Start each report with the outcome in one sentence a non-developer understands, then the details.
